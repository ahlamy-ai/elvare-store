# ÉLVARÉ — Worldwide mixed assortment launch plan
Version: 23 November 2026
Status: PRE-LAUNCH / NO CHECKOUT

## 1. Commercial positioning
ÉLVARÉ is a curated, premium-positioned women's fashion store. The assortment is mixed:
- Everyday wardrobe: dresses, tops, knitwear and coordinated pieces.
- Occasion: eveningwear and statement pieces.
- Finishing touches: bags, accessories and selected footwear.

Worldwide is the long-term ambition, not a promise that every destination is currently serviceable. Enable destinations only after supplier shipping, landed cost, customs/tax treatment, delivery estimates, returns route and payment settlement are verified for that country.

## 2. Product admission rules
Do not publish a product as purchasable until all checks pass:
1. Supplier SKU/EAN and variant identifiers are stable.
2. Product name, brand, composition/material, size chart, colour and care details are usable and accurate.
3. At least one usable image is available and supplier photo use is permitted.
4. Live stock is checked immediately before an order; XML feed snapshots are not live inventory (Matterhorn says feed files refresh about every two hours).
5. Supplier price, VAT treatment and destination-specific shipping cost are confirmed.
6. Brand resale/channel restrictions are checked.
7. Delivery lead time, customs responsibility and returns/defect process are disclosed.
8. Product has sufficient margin after every variable cost.

Initial curation target: 30–60 strong SKUs, not thousands of unreviewed feed entries. Prioritise dependable sizes, clear imagery, repeatable stock, lower return risk and shipping-friendly weights. Avoid fragile, unusually heavy, highly size-sensitive or unclear-brand products at launch.

Suggested initial mix (planning targets, not a statement about current stock):
- 45% everyday wardrobe
- 30% occasion
- 25% bags/accessories/selected footwear

## 3. Pricing policy
Never set customer prices by multiplying wholesale price alone. Calculate per SKU and destination:

landed variable cost =
supplier net item price
+ supplier shipping to that destination
+ supplier VAT or other supplier charges, if applicable
+ payment processing and FX fees
+ expected returns/refunds/chargebacks allowance
+ destination taxes/duties borne by ÉLVARÉ
+ variable fulfilment/customer-service cost.

For a target contribution margin before fixed overhead and income tax:

retail price = landed variable cost / (1 - target contribution margin)

Planning bands:
- Core everyday products: 25–30% contribution margin target.
- Occasion / differentiated products: 30–35%.
- Accessories / low-return add-ons: 30–40%.

These are internal targets, not guaranteed net profit. Validate the final price against genuine comparable retail offers and customer willingness to pay. If a product cannot support the target after shipping and fees, do not list it or use a clearly disclosed shipping charge. Do not hide shipping costs in a way that surprises buyers.

Illustrative example only:
- Item cost €40
- Destination shipping €10
- Payment/FX €3
- Returns/claims reserve €5
- Other variable taxes/charges €0 (only if verified)
- Landed variable cost €58
- At 30% contribution target: price €82.86
This is not a live Matterhorn SKU quote and excludes fixed overhead and income tax. If VAT/duties apply, recalculate before publishing.

Round prices only after calculating the margin. Keep a per-country price matrix where costs differ. Do not publish one universal shipping-inclusive price if shipping costs vary materially.

## 4. Destination rollout
Start with a country only when all of the following are confirmed:
- Matterhorn ships that SKU/brand to the destination.
- Current destination shipping price and delivery estimate are available.
- Import duties, VAT/GST and who pays them are clearly handled.
- Customer returns and defective-item process is workable.
- Payment processor accepts the Algerian seller and permitted business model.
- The bank confirms compliant collection and supplier-payment/foreign-exchange process.

Use three internal destination states:
- ENABLED: all checks documented; may accept orders after legal launch approval.
- QUOTE-ONLY: shipping/tax/payment terms not fully verified; do not accept orders.
- BLOCKED: supplier does not ship, product/brand restriction, or legal/payment issue.

No destination is enabled by this plan. Checkout remains disabled.

## 5. Free-first marketing plan
Before legal opening:
- Build an editorial Instagram, Pinterest and TikTok presence using supplier-approved images and original styling/copy.
- Publish useful content: outfit edits, capsule wardrobe combinations, occasion styling, colour edits and care guides.
- Create a clear brand story and consistent visual language; avoid fake reviews, fake scarcity, fake discounts and claims of stock/delivery that are not verified.
- Collect expressions of interest only if privacy notice and local legal requirements are satisfied; do not take deposits or pre-orders.
- Use organic search pages for categories and useful style guides; write original descriptions rather than copying supplier text wholesale.
- Contact micro-creators for unpaid product-independent collaborations only; do not promise free products or commissions that cannot be funded.
- Track reach, saves, qualified clicks, email opt-ins and product-interest signals. Do not buy traffic until conversion economics are known.

After legal/payment approval:
- Open only verified shipping destinations and SKUs.
- Start with a small organic launch, measure product-page views, add-to-cart, checkout completion, refunds and contribution margin.
- Test paid promotion only from a budget the business can afford to lose; never finance ads with customer funds or supplier money.
- Pause any SKU with stock mismatch, excessive returns, late delivery or negative contribution margin.

## 6. Customer trust and operations
Before accepting orders, publish accurate seller identity and contact details, privacy notice, terms, full price and shipping fees, delivery estimates, customs/tax treatment, cancellation/returns/refunds and complaint process.
Keep supplier invoices, customer invoices, payment settlement records, tracking proof, refund records and a per-order profitability ledger.
Matterhorn's dropshipping help states orders are processed after payment is received by the supplier; manual/API order placement may be required outside its dedicated Shopify app. Do not describe ÉLVARÉ as fully automated until a tested integration proves it.
Matterhorn also states feeds update approximately every two hours, destination shipping prices vary, packages can incur return/reship charges, and some brands have marketplace restrictions. Recheck its current official terms before launch.

## 7. Legal and payment launch gate
This plan does not grant legal approval. The owner must complete Algerian business registration and confirm the applicable e-commerce, domain/hosting, consumer-protection, tax, foreign-exchange and banking requirements for the exact third-country dropshipping flow.
Obtain written confirmation from the Algerian bank on:
- receiving customer proceeds from foreign buyers;
- domiciliation/repatriation requirements;
- paying a foreign supplier for goods shipped from one foreign country to another;
- required invoices, customs/shipping evidence and foreign-exchange documentation.

Obtain written merchant approval from the payment provider for an Algeria-resident seller, the actual legal entity, fashion/dropshipping activity and settlement account.
Do not use personal payment accounts as a workaround. Do not activate checkout, accept orders, deposits or pre-orders until these gates are passed.

## 8. Launch KPIs
Track weekly:
- qualified visitors by country;
- product-page engagement and saves;
- add-to-cart and checkout conversion (after launch);
- average order value;
- landed variable cost and contribution margin by SKU/country;
- on-time delivery, cancellation, refund and return rates;
- customer acquisition cost and repeat purchase rate.

North-star metric: positive contribution after product, shipping, payment/FX, taxes borne by seller, returns and acquisition costs — not gross sales.
