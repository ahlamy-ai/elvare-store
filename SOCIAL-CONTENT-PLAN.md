# ÉLVARÉ — Organic Social Content Plan

Status: prepared for production; no posts are scheduled or published by this document.

## Channels
- Instagram: @elvare.officia
- TikTok: @elvare.officia
- YouTube: ÉLVARÉ channel (Shorts)
- Timezone: Africa/Algiers

## Daily target
Five posts total per day across the three channels:
- 2 short-form videos (adapted for Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts)
- 3 product/editorial image posts

This is a production target, not a claim that the current free tools can generate or auto-publish five finished posts every day. Each product post requires an approved supplier image, written reuse permission, verified product details and a final price. YouTube Shorts and video posts require actual video files.

## Content pillars
1. Brand identity: French-inspired editorial fashion, Bourges concept story.
2. Styling education: capsule wardrobe, colour pairing, occasion styling.
3. Product spotlights: only supplier-approved items with confirmed price and availability.
4. Trust and transparency: explain the pre-launch process honestly.
5. Community: polls, questions and style preferences; never fabricate testimonials or sales.

## Caption framework
**Editorial:** “A considered point of view. Discover the ÉLVARÉ fashion concept — contemporary style, thoughtful details and a French-inspired perspective. Explore our pre-launch preview: https://ahlamy-ai.github.io/elvare-store/”

**Product spotlight (use only after verification):** “Meet [verified product name]. [Verified material/fit/detail]. Price: [approved final price]. Availability and delivery are confirmed at checkout once ÉLVARÉ officially opens. Explore the collection preview: https://ahlamy-ai.github.io/elvare-store/”

**Pre-launch CTA:** “Follow ÉLVARÉ as we curate the first edit. Our preview is open; orders and payments are not yet accepted.”

These are starting points, not guaranteed best times.

## Publishing rules
- Never invent product prices, stock, discounts, orders, reviews or customer counts.
- Never use supplier images until image and description reuse rights are confirmed.
- Do not write “Shop now”, “Order now” or imply checkout is open while the store is in preview.
- Keep paid promotion, subscriptions and paid credits disabled.
- Schedule only after the actual media file and caption are ready; verify each scheduled item in Metricool.
