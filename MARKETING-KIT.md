# ÉLVARÉ — Organic launch kit

## Positioning
A considered, premium-leaning fashion edit: modern everyday wardrobe, occasion dressing, bags, accessories and selected footwear. Worldwide is the long-term target; shipping destinations will be enabled only after landed costs, delivery performance, customs and returns are verified.

## Current public status
Pre-launch preview only. No checkout, orders, deposits or payments. Do not advertise products as available, quote unverified prices, claim delivery times, or imply the store is already taking orders.

## Brand voice
Quiet confidence, editorial, precise, warm. Avoid fake scarcity, fake reviews, inflated original prices and claims such as luxury/designer unless each item supports that claim.

## 14-day no-ad launch routine
- Days 1–2: Publish the teaser on Instagram, Facebook and Pinterest; complete profile descriptions and link to the preview.
- Days 3–5: Publish three category moodboards (everyday, occasion, accessories), clearly marked as inspiration rather than purchasable products.
- Days 6–8: Publish the brand philosophy and a short behind-the-scenes selection post.
- Days 9–11: Share practical styling ideas and outfit combinations without claiming the pictured items are in stock.
- Days 12–14: Review reach, saves, profile visits and outbound clicks; repeat the strongest topic.

## Launch gates before selling
1. Confirm the Algerian legal route and required seller disclosures.
2. Confirm the bank's permitted commercial receipt and supplier-payment route.
3. Confirm supplier account status, actual stock and product-use rights.
4. Verify shipping price and delivery estimate by destination and weight.
5. Calculate landed cost, taxes, payment/FX fees, return reserve and sustainable margin for every SKU/country.
6. Publish accurate privacy, returns, delivery, contact and seller details.
7. Only then enable payment and accept orders.
